Training Matrix

Not sure whose fire safety training runs out next month? See at a glance who is trained on what, and get early warning before training runs out.

For: Any business that trains staff: cafés, salons, care providers, workshops, clinics and small manufacturers.

A look inside

Example data shown. The template opens with example rows that you delete when you're ready.

What it does

  • Everyone down the side, every training across the top
  • Green for in date, amber for due soon, red for expired, grey for not done
  • Expiry dates worked out for you from how long each training lasts
  • Choose how many days' warning you get for each training
  • A full training history, with older records kept as 'Replaced'
  • A dashboard counting what's expired, due soon or not done

What you get

  • The Training Matrix template (.xlsx)
  • A step-by-step set-up guide (PDF)

Works with

Microsoft Excel (2019 or later, or Microsoft 365), or Google Sheets (free).

Room for

50 people, 20 trainings and 500 training records.
