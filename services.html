What we do

Six ways we help small businesses spend less time on admin. Pick one, or put them together.

WebsitesBookings and client adminCustom business toolsProcess and automationQuality and compliance toolsOngoing care
Websites

People can't find you, or aren't sure about what they find.

A clear, fast website that explains what you do, works on any phone and gets found on Google.

For example

  • A website that explains what you do in plain words
  • Set up so it can be found on Google, with your Google Business Profile
  • An enquiry form that sends messages straight to your inbox
  • Your own web address and matching email
Bookings and client admin

Too much time chasing bookings, forms and payments.

Online booking, payment at booking, intake forms and reminder emails that run themselves.

For example

  • Clients book real diary slots and pay in one step
  • Automatic reminders that cut no-shows
  • Intake and consent forms completed before the first appointment
  • Video call links created for online sessions
Custom business tools

Your business runs on spreadsheets only you understand.

Dashboards, trackers, calculators and portals, built around how your business actually works.

For example

  • A shared tracker your whole team can tick off
  • A quote or price builder that works out totals for you
  • A dashboard of the numbers you check every week
  • Stock and reorder tracking
Process and automation

The same admin, every day, done by hand.

We map how work flows through your business, cut the steps you don't need and join your tools up.

For example

  • A clear map of how a job moves from enquiry to invoice
  • A written list of quick wins to save time
  • Enquiries that file themselves and get an instant reply
  • Fewer copy-and-paste jobs between systems
Quality and compliance tools

Paperwork that's hard to find and harder to keep up to date.

Procedure libraries, document control, training records and audit trails, kept in order.

For example

  • One home for every procedure, always the latest version
  • A training matrix showing who's trained on what, and what's due
  • Review dates and change history for your documents
  • Daily check logs and issue logs with a clear audit trail
Ongoing care

Worried about what happens after it goes live.

Hosting, updates, friendly support and a regular check-in to keep things improving.

For example

  • Small changes made for you
  • Help when something stops working
  • Your tools and accounts kept up to date
  • A regular look at what to improve next
