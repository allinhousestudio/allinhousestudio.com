Questions and answers

Can't find what you need? Get in touch and we'll help.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Small businesses of all kinds: therapists and clinics, cafés and food businesses, trades, salons, workshops and small manufacturers. If admin is eating your week, we'd like to hear from you.

I'm not very technical. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We explain everything in plain English, and every handover comes with a step-by-step guide.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so we agree a fixed price before we start. Tell us what you need and we'll suggest a plan.

Who owns the website and accounts?

You do. We set everything up in your name, so your website, booking system and data stay yours.

Can you work with the tools I already use?

Often, yes. We'll look at what you have first and only suggest changes where they'll save you time.

What happens after it's finished?

You get a simple guide and a walkthrough. If you'd like, we can look after things for you every month: small changes, help when something stops working, and a regular check-in.

Can you help me get found on Google?

Yes. We set your website up so Google can find it, and help with your Google Business Profile.

Will your quality and compliance tools make my business compliant?

No tool can do that on its own. Our tools help you keep clear, organised records, but they don't tell you what your industry requires. Check that with your regulator or an adviser.

Where are you based?

Scotland. We work with businesses across the UK, mostly online.
