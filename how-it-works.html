Simple, from start to finish

No jargon and no surprises. Here's what working with us looks like.

  1. 1Tell us what's slowing you downA short, no-pressure chat about your business and where the time goes.
  2. 2We suggest a planA clear, written plan with a fixed price, so there are no surprises.

What you can expect

  • A fixed price. Agreed before we start, so you know where you stand.
  • Everything in your name. Your website, accounts and data belong to you.
  • Plain English. We explain things simply and put it in writing.
  • Instructions anyone can follow. One step at a time, exactly what to click, and how to check it worked.
  • Help afterwards. Ongoing care if you'd like us to keep things running smoothly.
Example from a handover guide

Block out a holiday

  1. Open your booking system and click Availability.
  2. Click Block off time.
  3. Choose the dates and click Save.

Why: clients can't book while you're away.

How to check it worked: those dates show as unavailable on your booking page.
