Who we are

AllInHouse Studio is a small business based in Scotland, UK. We're responsible for the personal details you give us through this website. Contact us at morag@allinhousestudio.com.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, business name, the areas you're interested in and your message.
  • Website use: if you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the site, such as pages visited, the device and browser you use, and roughly where you are. See our cookies page.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

What we doLegal basis
Reply to your enquiryYour consent, and our legitimate interest in answering questions about our business
Understand how the website is used (Google Analytics)Your consent, given through the cookie banner

Who we share it with

We never sell your details. We only use trusted services that help us run the website:

  • Formspree, which delivers form messages to our inbox. Formspree is based in the USA and protects data sent there with standard contractual safeguards.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website and may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Our email provider, which stores the messages we send and receive.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: up to 2 years after we last spoke, unless you become a client.
  • Google Analytics: up to 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, object to how we use them, or withdraw your consent at any time. Email morag@allinhousestudio.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
