Team Task Tracker

Tasks living in emails, notebooks and someone's head? One shared list of everything your team needs to do, with progress for each stage and overdue tasks flagged in red.

For: Small teams launching something new, running a project, or juggling lots of moving parts.

A look inside

Example data shown. The template opens with example rows that you delete when you're ready.

What it does

  • Every task with a stage, an owner and a due date
  • Overdue tasks turn red; finished tasks turn grey
  • Hide finished tasks, or show one person's tasks, in two clicks
  • A dashboard with progress bars for each stage
  • Overall progress, plus how many tasks are blocked or overdue
  • Your own stage names and team names in simple drop-down lists

What you get

  • The Team Task Tracker template (.xlsx)
  • A step-by-step set-up guide (PDF)

Works with

Microsoft Excel (2019 or later, or Microsoft 365), or Google Sheets (free).

Room for

200 tasks, 10 stages and 10 team members.
