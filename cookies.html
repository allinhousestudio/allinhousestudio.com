Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. We keep them to a minimum.

Cookies we always use

We save one small note in your browser to remember whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies, so we don't ask every time. It doesn't identify you and isn't shared with anyone.

If you click Accept, we use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they find useful. If you click Decline, Google Analytics doesn't load at all.
