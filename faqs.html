Questions and answers

Can't find what you need? Get in touch and we'll help.

Which software do I need?

Microsoft Excel (2019 or later, or Microsoft 365) or Google Sheets, which is free with a Google account. The set-up guides cover both.

Do the templates work on a Mac?

Yes. Use Excel for Mac or Google Sheets in your web browser.

Do I need to be good with spreadsheets?

No. You type into the clearly marked cream cells, and everything else works itself out. The guide takes you through it one step at a time.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Share it from Google Sheets, or save it in OneDrive or SharePoint for Excel. Anyone in your business can use it. See the template licence for the details.

Can I change the template?

Yes. It's yours to adapt for use in your own business. You just can't resell or share it outside your business.

Where is my data kept?

In your own Excel file or Google account. We never see it.

When can I buy the templates?

Our shop opens soon. Leave your email on any template page and we'll tell you when it's ready.

Will a template make my business compliant?

No template can do that on its own. Our quality and compliance templates help you keep clear, organised records, but they don't tell you what your industry requires. Check that with your regulator or an adviser.

Can you set a template up for me?

Yes. Get in touch and we'll set it up with your details and walk you through it.

Do you build websites and custom tools?

Yes. Tell us what's slowing you down and we'll suggest a plan with a fixed price.
