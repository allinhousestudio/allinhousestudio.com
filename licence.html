Template licence

What you can and can't do with a template you buy from us. Last updated {UPDATED}.

You can

  • Use the template in your own business, for as long as you like.
  • Let anyone who works in or for your business use it.
  • Change it to suit your business: add columns, rename things, change colours.
  • Make copies for use within your business, including backups.

You can't

  • Sell, give away or share the template or its guide outside your business.
  • Upload it to a website, marketplace or file-sharing service.
  • Use it, or a copy of it, as part of a product or service you sell to others.
  • Remove our name from the guide.

Using it for more than one business

Each business needs its own copy. If you run several businesses, or you're an adviser who'd like to use our templates with clients, get in touch and we'll sort out the right licence.

Good to know

  • We own the copyright in our templates and guides. Buying a template gives you a licence to use it, not ownership of it.
  • The templates help you keep clear, organised records. They don't tell you what your industry or regulator requires, and you're responsible for the information you put into them.
