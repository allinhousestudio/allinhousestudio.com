Case study · Therapy practice

Claire Ratcliffe Psychotherapy

An online therapy practice for individuals, couples and therapists, across the UK and worldwide. Claire needed a website that clients could find, trust and book from, without her spending hours on admin.

Visit the live website ↗

What we built

Everything set up in Claire's name, so it all stays hers.

Websites

A 15-page website

  • Built on her own web address, with the old address redirected to it
  • Calm, editorial design that works on phones, tablets and computers
  • Separate pages for therapy, LGBTQIA+ affirming therapy, therapists, fees and FAQs
Bookings and client admin

Online booking

  • 16 session types, each bookable only at her real diary times
  • Intake form completed at the first booking
  • Times shown in each client's own time zone, with Zoom links created automatically
Custom business tools

Payments and group bookings

  • Card payment at booking, with automatic receipts
  • Refund and cancellation rules built into the reminders
  • Six-month supervision groups with their own booking and confirmation emails
Process and automation

Enquiries and mailing list

  • An enquiry form that goes straight to her inbox
  • A mailing list that keeps therapists and clients separate
  • Sign-up forms across the site
Getting found

Google and directories

  • Google Search Console and Bing set up and checked
  • Google Business Profile set up
  • Page titles written for the searches her clients make
Ongoing care

Guides and weekly reports

  • Step-by-step guides to her tools and logins
  • Pricing one-pagers and a printable supervision flyer
  • A weekly website scorecard with clear next steps
After launch

Weekly website reports

A website only helps if people find it. Each week we turn the numbers into a one-page scorecard that says what's working, what isn't and what to do next, in plain English.

  • How many people visited, and where they came from: Google, social media or directories.
  • What people searched for on Google, and where the site appeared.
  • Enquiries and bookings that came through the website.
  • Clear suggestions, with who does what, and what to watch next week.
