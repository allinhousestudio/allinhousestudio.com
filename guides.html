Set-up guides anyone can follow

Every template comes with its own step-by-step guide, included when you buy. Here's what they're like.

How our guides work

  • One action per step. No long paragraphs to wade through.
  • Exactly what to click. We name the button, tab and cell.
  • Why it matters, in one short line after the steps.
  • A check at the end of every task, so you know it worked.
  • Steps for both Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.
Example from a guide

Hide finished tasks

  1. On the Tasks tab, click the small arrow in the Status heading.
  2. Untick Done.
  3. Click OK.

Why: keeps the list short, without deleting anything.

How to check it worked: only unfinished tasks are showing.
